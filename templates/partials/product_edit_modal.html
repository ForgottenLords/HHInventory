{% comment %} Edit form for a product, shared by the Product Library and the product page. Belongs inside the Vue root; its styles and behaviour come from product_edit_modal_assets.html, which belongs in . An including page has to mix productEditForm into its app and define onProductSaved(), to say what it refreshes once the edit lands. It also has to supply the labels that product_labels() in core/views/pages.py builds. Open the form with askEditProduct(id). Validation is server-side: submit always reaches UpdateProduct, and fieldErrors / formError show the response. {% endcomment %}
