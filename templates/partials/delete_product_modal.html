{% comment %} Confirmation dialog for deleting a product, shared by the Product Library and the product page. Belongs inside the Vue root; its styles and behaviour come from delete_product_modal_assets.html, which belongs in . An including page has to mix deleteProductDialog into its app and define onProductDeleted(), to say what replaces the product once it is gone. It also has to offer formatPrice(), and labels for name, brand, barcode and estimated_price. Open the dialog with askDeleteProduct(product), passing a product that carries id, name, productType, brand, barcode and estimatedPrice. {% endcomment %}
